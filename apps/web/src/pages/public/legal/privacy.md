## 2. What We Collect

### 2.1 Information you give us
- **Account data:** name, email address, password (hashed — we never store it in plain text), or, if you sign in via Google/Microsoft, the name, email, and verification status those providers share with us.
- **Billing data:** we do not store your card details. Payment processing is handled by Stripe; we retain billing metadata such as plan, subscription status, and payment history (amounts, dates, status) for your Account Settings → Billing page.
- **Content you create:** RCA text, attachments, comments, and any other content you input ("Your Content," described above).
- **Communications:** if you contact support, we keep a record of that correspondence.

### 2.2 Information collected automatically
- **Usage data:** pages visited, actions taken (e.g., exports, invites — used to populate your own Security/Audit log), timestamps, and approximate device/browser information (user agent).
- **Log and security data:** IP address, login timestamps, and device information, retained for security, fraud prevention, and the audit log features described in the Service.
- **Cookies:** we use only essential cookies/local storage required for login sessions and core functionality. [UPDATE IF YOU ADD ANALYTICS/MARKETING COOKIES — if so, you'll need a cookie consent banner for EU/UK users.]

**We do not sell your personal data.** We do not use Your Content (the RCAs you write) to train AI models or for advertising.

## 7. Data Retention

- **Active accounts:** we retain your data for as long as your account is active.
- **Account deletion:** when you delete your account (Account Settings → Delete my account), your account is disabled immediately. Your personal data and files are permanently deleted after a **14-day grace period**, except where we're required to retain certain records (e.g., billing/tax records) for longer under applicable law.
- **Shared workspace data:** if you're the sole owner of a shared workspace, you'll be asked to transfer ownership or delete the workspace before your account can be deleted, since other collaborators' access depends on it.
- **Backups:** deleted data may persist in encrypted backups for a limited additional period before being purged, per our backup rotation schedule.
